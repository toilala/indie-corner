Play Small Games Directly in Your Browser

Welcome to our lightweight indie arcade. We created three classic mini-games built completely with standard HTML5 Canvas, modern CSS layouts, and plain JavaScript without loading any external engines or game frameworks. Select a game below, hit start, and try to set a high score.

CURRENT GAME: Asteroid Dodge
SCORE: 0 | HIGH: 0
Controls: Left / Right keys or mouse. Collect blue squares (+10), avoid red rocks.

1. Asteroid Dodge

Control your craft at the bottom of the screen. Slide left and right to intercept drifting blue power cores for points while dodging incoming red meteorites.

2. Brick Bounce

A single-player breakout challenge. Keep the white ball in play using your paddle, aiming to clear every row of bricks without letting the ball fall past your paddle.

3. Classic Snake

Navigate your snake across the grid using the arrow keys. Eat the red apples to grow longer and increase your score, but make sure not to crash into the walls or your own tail.
